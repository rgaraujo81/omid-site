    Data sovereignty

    A cloud is only as sovereign
    as the law that protects it.

    Encryption does not decide jurisdiction. What decides it is where the data physically sits and which legislation reaches whoever operates it. At OMID there is a single answer: Brazil.

    01 We do not sell, use or transfer your data. Your data does not become raw material for our products, for model training or for third-party deals. It is yours and it stays yours.
    02 Brazilian jurisdiction, end to end. Governed by the LGPD and the Marco Civil da Internet, under Brazilian courts. No exposure to the US CLOUD Act or to foreign authority requests.
    03 Infrastructure, operations and support inside the country. The data center is ours, the NOC is ours, and whoever answers your ticket lives here. No layer is handed off abroad.
